# UX guide: make it feel great

## Feel
Calm, warm, encouraging. A patient teacher beside you, not an arcade.

## Visual language
| Element | Style |
|---|---|
| Fretboard overlay | Thin, soft white lines at 40% opacity; frets slightly brighter than strings |
| Ghost hand / finger dots | Translucent cyan; number in each dot (1–4); gentle breathing pulse |
| Correct | Soft green + ✓ shape |
| Needs work | Warm amber + ! shape (never red for mistakes) |
| Skip string | × above the string; open string ○ |
| Panels | Rounded, dark glass, large text, always facing the player, within arm's reach beside the guitar body |

Colour is never the only signal: always pair it with a shape or text.

## Motion
- Ease in/out, 200–300 ms; nothing snaps or flashes.
- Ghost hand morphs to the next chord one beat early.
- Celebrations are small: a short sparkle by the strings, not full-screen.

## Sound
- Soft wooden metronome click.
- Gentle chime on clean chord; low, friendly "hmm" tone on a miss (never a buzzer).
- Keep app sounds quieter than the guitar.

## Words
- Short, specific, kind: "The G string is muted. Arch your finger a little."
- One tip at a time. Praise specific things: "Clean change from Em to D!"
- Never: "Wrong!", "Fail", scores in red.

## First five minutes
Fit → tune → first chord → celebration, with no menus in between.

## Comfort
- Seated only; everything within reach; no locomotion.
- Suggest a break after ~15 minutes.
- Pause anywhere: palm up (no pinch) or stop playing for 10 seconds.
